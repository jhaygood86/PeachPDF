Scroll-driven animations

The bar on top follows how far the page is scrolled. Each card slides in as it enters the view, and the swatches change colour while they cross it. Nothing here runs on time.

animation-timeline: scroll()The progress bar scales with the document's scroll position.
animation-timeline: view()This card is tied to how much of itself is in view.
animation-range: entry 0% entry 100%It plays over the part of the scroll where the card is entering.
cover 0% cover 100%The swatch plays over the whole time the element crosses the view.
Scrolling backNothing ever finishes: scroll up and it plays backwards.
