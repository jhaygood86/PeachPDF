# `contain` / `content-visibility: auto` now take effect, and `legend` is a block

- **Before:** `contain` and `content-visibility` were ignored (an unknown property), so a box with `contain: layout` or `paint` did not contain its floats, did not clip, was not a containing block for absolute/fixed descendants and was not a stacking context. A `position: fixed` box inside one was placed against the page. `body`'s background and overflow always propagated to the canvas and viewport. A `<legend>` was inline.
- **Now:** layout and paint containment (alone, through `content`/`strict`, or through `content-visibility: auto`) give the box a formatting context of its own, make it the containing block of its absolute and fixed descendants, create a stacking context, and (paint) clip to the padding edge. On `html` or `body` they stop `body`'s background and overflow propagating. A `<legend>` is a block with 2px inline padding, so a fieldset's legend now sits on a line of its own at the top.
- **Check with** `git show <previous-tag>:docs/html-css-support.md`: the previous page listed `contain` as unsupported.
- **Size and style containment, `contain-intrinsic-size` and `content-visibility: hidden` do something too:** a `contain: size` or `strict` box is as tall as an empty one (plus padding, border and its `contain-intrinsic-size`) and its contents overflow it (clipped with `strict`); counters and quotes inside a `contain: style` box are scoped to it; `content-visibility: hidden` leaves its contents out of the document.
- **A `display: none` box no longer takes part in counting through a following sibling's walk**, which could hand the counter of a hidden last child to what follows.
- **`contain: inline-size`, `overflow-clip-margin` and `content-visibility: auto` on a screen exist:** an inline-size-contained box is as wide as an empty one; `overflow: clip` and paint containment clip at the edge `overflow-clip-margin` names; a `Screen` document that sets `HtmlDocumentOptions.SkipOffscreenContent` skips what is off screen.
- **`container-type: size|inline-size` now applies style containment and size or inline-size containment to the container:** a shrink-to-fit container (a float, an inline-block, an absolutely positioned or flex item with an `auto` width) is as wide as an empty box rather than as wide as its contents, an auto-height `container-type: size` container is as tall as one, and counters and quotes inside it are scoped to it.
- **Still not done:** the native views do not call `UpdateContentVisibilityAsync`; the per-side `overflow-clip-margin` longhands.
