# `clear` does not see floats that come before an ancestor of the cleared box

`CssLayoutEngine.ClearBox` scans the earlier children of the box's own parent, and (through `LowestFloatBottomIn`) everything inside them down to a formatting context of its own; it does not look at the earlier siblings of the parent or of any other ancestor. A `clear` in a wrapper that follows a float (`<div style="float:left">`, then `<div><div style="clear:left">`) is not cleared: Chrome y=70, here y=0; a cleared float there is placed beside the first one. CSS 2.1 section 9.5.2 has it clear every float of its block formatting context that comes before it (tracking issue #2239). The rule 5 lookup `DomUtils.GetLowestEarlierFloatTop` already walks up the ancestors and is the model for it, with the relative offsets, formatting contexts and multi-column containers it handles.
