# A multi-column container of definite height does not make overflow columns

A container with a definite `height` fills columns of exactly that height, so content that does not fit one column starts the next (css-multicol-1 section 7; fixed for #2238). What does not fit the **last** column of a container that does not clip continues on the next page, as before, instead of in overflow columns to the right (section 3.3, which Chrome makes: 120 words in `columns:2; height:100px; column-fill:auto` fill further columns beyond the container). Measured when tried: with overflow columns a document whose container spans pages (`FlexContainerInsideAMultiColumnContainer_FillsItsColumnOnEveryPage`) lost its later pages' columns off the page's right edge, so the page-continuation behavior stays. A container that clips (`overflow` other than `visible`) drops the rest (#1818).
