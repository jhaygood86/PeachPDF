# A left float after an inline-block on the same line is drawn over it

A browser lays the line out again around a left float placed after an `inline-block` (`inline-flex`, `inline-grid`, `inline-table`): the box moves right if it fits beside the float, and the float goes below the line if it does not. Here the float is placed at the line start over the box in both cases. The words on a line are moved beside such a float (`ShiftLineBesideFloat`), but a box already placed with content of its own is not. A right float after one goes below the line (`FloatOverlapsLineBox` and the blocker scan), which needs nothing moved (tracking issue #2227). The right-float rule also stops at the box's siblings: an inline-block inside an earlier inline wrapper (`<span><span style="display:inline-block"><div>`) that holds block content does not send a right float below the line, and a line that cannot wrap (`white-space: nowrap`) with an inline-block that holds only text leaves it over the box. The float is placed below the line's bottom as it stands when it is met, so content after it on the line that makes the line taller is not counted, and an inline-block with a `margin-top` lands about 20px short of Chrome with a 10px margin.
