# Break avoidance between flex lines or grid rows

A forced break at the break point between two flex lines or grid rows is taken from either side since issue #441 — `break-after` on the earlier line's items or `break-before` on the later line's. **Avoidance is not**: `LineMayNotBeCut` (both engines) reads only `break-inside` and §2 monolithic content, so `break-after: avoid` on one line's items and `break-before: avoid` on the next line's say nothing, and the boundary falls between the two lines regardless. Measured on a 200pt page: two full-width 40pt flex lines, the first declaring `break-after: avoid`, land in slots 0 and 1 — the break the `avoid` forbids. The user-agent print sheet's `h1-h6 { page-break-after: avoid }` makes this the ordinary case for a heading that is a flex item.

Avoidance is a genuinely different shape from a forced break, which is why it was not folded into #441: a forced break moves the *later* line, while an avoided one has to move the **earlier** line too — that is keep-with-next, with §4.3's progressive relaxation, and the block-flow answer (`EarlyBreak.Discover` + `DomUtils.GetPrecedingKeepWithNextRun`) reads a chain of siblings rather than a chain of lines. Filed as [issue #447](https://github.com/jhaygood86/PeachPDF/issues/447).
