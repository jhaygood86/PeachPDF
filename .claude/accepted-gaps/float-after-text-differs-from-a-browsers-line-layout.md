# A float after text lands differently from a browser's re-laid line (#1784, #1785, #1786)

**Gap:** three float cases still differ from Chrome in word position. Measured by laying the same numbered words out in both and comparing every word's position (3pt across, 6pt down).

- **A float that follows text on its line shifts the words rather than re-laying the line** (#1784). `CssLayoutEngine.FlowFloatChild` places the float at the line top, then `ShiftLineBesideFloat` moves the words already on the line beside it when they fit; otherwise the float starts the next line and the words after it start that line too, where a browser keeps them on the current one. Under `white-space: nowrap` a browser keeps the run on one line (30 words differ on `main` and now alike). Two left floats after the same text: 64 of 70 words differ now, against 61 on `main`. A float that reaches the middle of the line is better than on `main` (41 words differ now, 76 on `main`).
- **`shape-outside` is not supported** (#1785). Text wraps around the float's rectangle (on the reviewed documents 7 words differ on `main` and 15 now, because the shift rule moves words against the float's rectangle). It matters here only because a shaped float leaves the shift rule above working against a rectangle the browser does not use.
- **A first-child float ignores its negative `margin-top`** (#1786). Block flow gives such a float no margin (it collapses into the parent), so a second left float with a larger negative `margin-top` is placed over the first. A right float starts from the position it was given, so a second right float sits beside the first, as in a browser. `CssFloatCoordinates.OuterTop` reads the position as border-box top for a left float and as margin-edge top for a right float; both match the layout's behaviour on `main` where the search terminates.

**Why out of scope:** re-laying the line with the float placed first is a rewrite of the inline flow around floats, not a fix to the box generation this change makes (a box-generation step that lets the columns engine see text). The shift rule means a float never covers a word, which `main` allowed. `shape-outside` needs a shape-aware line extent in `ExtentBesideFloats`; the first-child margin is a float-margin collapse bug in block flow that predates this change.

**Overall:** on the reviewer's 324 generated float-among-text documents, compared with Chrome by word position, the shift rule was better on 134, worse on 22 and equal on 148.
