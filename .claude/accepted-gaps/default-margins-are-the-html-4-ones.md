# The default margins of p, h2-h6, blockquote, lists, fieldset and form are the HTML 4 ones

`CssDefaults.cs` gives `p`, `blockquote`, `ul`, `ol`, `dl`, `menu`, `fieldset`, `form` and `h4` a `1.12em` block margin, and `h2`, `h3`, `h5` and `h6` `.75em`, `.83em`, `1.5em` and `1.67em`. The HTML Standard's rendering section (and Chrome) has `1em` for `p`, `blockquote`, `ul`, `ol`, `dl`, `menu` and `pre`, `.83em`, `1em`, `1.33em`, `1.67em` and `2.33em` for `h2` to `h6`, and none for `fieldset` and `form` (tracking issue #2251). Only `h1` matches; `pre` differs too (15px above and none below, where Chrome has 14px on both sides in the measured sample). Changing them moves the content of every document that sets no margins of its own, and the page count of some, so it is the maintainers' decision.
