# The default margins of p, h2-h6, blockquote, lists, fieldset and form are the HTML 4 ones

`CssDefaults.cs` gives `p`, `blockquote`, `ul`, `ol`, `dl`, `menu`, `h4`, `fieldset` and `form` a `1.12em` block margin and `h2`-`h6` `.75em`, `.83em`, `1.12em`, `1.5em` and `1.67em`; the HTML Standard's rendering section (and Chrome) has `1em` for the first group, `.83em`, `1em`, `1.33em`, `1.67em` and `2.33em` for `h2`-`h6`, and none for `fieldset` and `form` (tracking issue #2251). Only `h1` matches. Changing them moves the content of every document that sets no margins of its own, and the page count of some, so it is the maintainers' decision.
