# A box split at a column boundary leaves that edge open

_Landed 2026-07-26._

**A box split at a column boundary leaves that edge open** ([issue #368](https://github.com/jhaygood86/PeachPDF/issues/368), [CSS Fragmentation 3 §6.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-break-3/#break-decoration)). `SliceGeometry` carried `HasLeftEdge`/`HasRightEdge` and no block-axis pair, because until #366 a block-level box was never sliced in the block axis without a page clip to cut its border away. A column has no clip — two columns share one page band — so a split box drew a **closed** border on each fragment: `clone`'s rendering under the `slice` value.

**The load-bearing idea is that the block pair cannot be derived the way the inline pair is, and the issue's own sizing has this backwards.** "Compare the fragment's rectangle against the whole box's" is exactly what does not work here: two column fragments of one box occupy the *same* block-axis range, so no comparison of rectangles can tell the box's own top from a column boundary. The honest source is the **break record** — which boxes layout said carry on past a fragmentainer — and it exists in two places, so both are consulted: the pass's own resumption chain (`_continuedFrom`/`_continuesInto`) for the page grid, and `NestedFragmentainer.Continuing` plus a new `ContinuedFrom` (the previous column's `Continuing`) for a column. A box resuming a *page* into the first column of the next needs both at once, which is why they are ORed rather than switched between.

**A break edge also has to have a fragment on the other side of it, and that half was found only by the showcase diff.** The record is not retracted when a §4.3 mover relocates a box *after* the pass that recorded it: `paged_media_monolithic_content`'s `overflow:hidden` card had begun flowing across the boundary, was laid out again whole on the next page, and its earlier fragment un-emitted (`InvalidateFrom`) — but it was still recorded as continuing, so reading the record alone **removed that card's own top border**. New `_fragmentRange` (per box, ignoring which nested fragmentainer, since two columns of one slot are two `FragmentKey`s) answers "is there a fragment before/after this one", and the two guards together are what make the flags right. Verified load-bearing by neutralizing each individually; the mover case needs a fixture whose page boundary falls *inside* the card's text, found by sweeping the filler count.

Paint consumes the pair symmetrically with the inline one: `BoxDecorationGeometry` carries all four, `BordersDrawHandler.DrawBoxBorders` gates the top and bottom edges and — the part that is easy to miss — `SetInOutsetRectanglePoints` stops mitring the *side* borders into an edge that is not there, which is the same rule `isLineStart` already applied on the other axis. `clone` forces all four true, as it must: closing each fragment is precisely what it asks for, and it is only distinguishable from `slice` at a column break now that `slice` has stopped doing it too.

An inline box broken across **lines** keeps both block edges on every rectangle, deliberately: it is not sliced in that axis at all — its top and bottom borders belong to each line it appears on, which is what a wrapping inline looks like in every UA.

Tests: `FragmentEmitterTests` (+5 — the column case, the page case, the relocated-box guard, an unfragmented box, and the wrapping inline), `BoxDecorationBreakPaintIntegrationTests` (+2 — however many fragments the paragraph splits into, `slice` draws exactly one top and one bottom edge while `clone` draws two per fragment). Full net8.0 suite green (6320); **100% diff coverage**. **62 of 63 showcases are pixel-identical**; `multicol` differs, and it differs by losing the two borders §6.2 says are not there, **verified in both PDFium and MuPDF**. **Accepted gap:** a `slice` decoration defined over the *whole* box — `border-radius`, a gradient/image background layer, a `box-shadow` — is still resolved against the fragment's own block extent at a column break, because a nested fragment's bounds are its own while a page fragment's are the whole box, so one strip rule cannot serve both and the concatenation wants a measuring pre-pass over the drafts ([#376](https://github.com/jhaygood86/PeachPDF/issues/376) — **now fixed**, see the Recent-fixes entry). This is also what #335's multicol half was waiting on: `clone` is now expressible at a column boundary at all.
