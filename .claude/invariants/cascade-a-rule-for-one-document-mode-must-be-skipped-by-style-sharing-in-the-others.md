# A user-agent rule that applies to one document mode only must not cost the other modes their style sharing

`CssData.CanShareStyleAmongSiblings` asks every candidate rule of an element (the universal ones and those under its tag, classes and id) whether it is stable, **whatever its `@media`**. The quirks block of the user-agent sheet (`@media (-peachpdf-quirks-mode)`) has structural selectors (`-peachpdf-no-substantial-previous` and the others, which read the siblings) whose subject has no tag of its own, so they live in the universal bucket. Unmarked, they made every element of every document, in standards mode too, unable to take a sibling's style: the cascade was slower for a rule that cannot match.

`IndexedRule.QuirksOnly` (`MediaQueryMatcher.RequiresQuirksMode` over the enclosing media) lets `AllowsSharedStyle` pass over such a rule when the document is not in quirks mode. **A new rule block that is conditional on a document mode and has an unstable selector needs the same flag, or has to give its subject a tag.** The symptom is not a wrong result but sibling style sharing quietly turning off: `OnlyTheQuirksBlockOfTheUserAgentSheetIsQuirksOnlyForStyleSharing` is the test.
