CcmpLigatureTest.ttf is an original, hand-authored test fixture generated by
generate_ccmp_ligature_font.py (in this directory). It contains no third-party
font data and is released into the public domain (CC0). It exists solely to
exercise PeachPDF's GSUB `ccmp` application and Default_Ignorable_Code_Point
handling in the test suite; it is not intended for text rendering.
