NotoColorEmojiSequences-Subset.ttf is a subset of the COLRv1 build of "Noto
Color Emoji", Copyright 2013 Google LLC, licensed under the SIL Open Font
License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1). It uses COLR (version 1) + CPAL color glyphs
over `glyf` outlines (no bitmap tables).

Unlike NotoColorEmoji-Subset.ttf (single-codepoint color glyphs, no GSUB), this
subset retains the font's `ccmp` feature and the glyphs needed for
multi-codepoint emoji *sequences* - ZWJ sequences, regional-indicator pairs and
tag sequences - whose composed glyph comes from GSUB rather than the cmap.

The font is bundled solely as a test/showcase asset to demonstrate and verify
emoji-sequence composition and COLR/CPAL color-glyph rendering. Only a small
range of glyphs is retained via fonttools subset (see
generate_color_emoji_sequences_subset.py); the color glyphs are otherwise
unmodified.
