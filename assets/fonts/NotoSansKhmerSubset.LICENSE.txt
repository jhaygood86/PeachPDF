NotoSansKhmerSubset.ttf is a subset (the consonants KA/RO/SA/NO/MO, COENG, four dependent vowel
signs (E/I/U/AA, one per reorder-relevant position), the independent vowel QA, SIGN TOANDAKHIAT
(the Xgroup category), SIGN REAHMUK (the Ygroup category), SIGN ROBAT (the Robatic category), KHAN,
two digits, common punctuation, digits, space, and the basic Latin alphabet) of the "Noto Sans
Khmer" font, Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/khmer), licensed
under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1). Instantiated to a single static (Regular
weight, normal width) instance from the upstream variable font before subsetting.

The font is bundled solely as a showcase/test asset so Khmer's coeng/subjoined-consonant shaping
(issue #1493) - GSUB locl/ccmp/pref/blwf/abvf/pstf/pres/abvs/blws/psts substitution and the
resulting glyph reorder (coeng+RO repositioning before the base, pre-base vowel-sign movement) -
renders with real, readable Khmer glyphs and can be verified by rasterizing actual output, rather
than only unit-tested against synthetic byte-blob GSUB tables. Only the glyphs the showcase/tests
actually use are retained, with every GSUB/GPOS layout feature kept intact; the glyph outlines
themselves are unmodified.
