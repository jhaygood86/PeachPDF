HintingCff2.otf, HintingCff2Single.otf, HintingCff2NoAxes.otf, HintingCff2Boxes.otf, HintingCff2.golden.json.gz and
HintingCff2Variants.golden.json.gz are synthetic test data made by generate_hinting_cff2_fixtures.py and generate_hinting_cff2_boxes.py
(with hinting_cff2_builder.py and hinting_cff2_variants.py) in this directory: fonts with CFF2 (variable CFF) outlines whose glyphs are random
Type 2 charstrings or rectangles, and small fonts whose tables are wrong in one deliberate way. They contain no third-party design and are
dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The golden data (HintingCff2.golden.json.gz) also holds coordinates that FreeType computed from the outlines and hints of the bundled font
VariableCff2Test.otf, and no font data. That font keeps its own license notice beside it (VariableCff2Test.LICENSE.txt).
