NotoSansThaiSubset.ttf and NotoSansLaoSubset.ttf are subsets of the "Noto Sans Thai" and "Noto Sans Lao" fonts:

  Noto Sans Thai  Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/thai)
  Noto Sans Lao   Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/lao)

licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1). Each was instantiated to a single static (Regular weight, normal width)
instance from the upstream variable font and cut down to the characters of the paragraphs of the dictionary line breaking showcase and a
few punctuation marks, digits, space and the basic Latin alphabet, with every GSUB/GPOS layout feature kept intact; the glyph outlines
themselves are unmodified (generate_southeast_asian_subsets.py, in this directory, makes them).

They are bundled solely as showcase assets, so that the dictionary line breaking of Thai and Lao is shown with real, readable text.
Nothing here ships in the PeachPDF library or its NuGet packages.
