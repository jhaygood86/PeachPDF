NotoSansCJK-Subset-CFF.cff.gz is a subset CFF program from Noto Sans CJK SC,
Copyright 2014-2021 Adobe (http://www.adobe.com/), licensed under the SIL Open
Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1).

The fixture is the embedded CID-keyed CFF extracted from the after-subset PDF
in the evidence release for PeachPDF PR #1660. It keeps the glyphs and
subroutines used by the example document and is bundled solely to make the CFF
subsetter tests deterministic across operating systems.
