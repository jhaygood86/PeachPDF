Noto is a trademark of Google Inc. It is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1).

The fixture is the CFF program taken from the embedded font stream of a PDF written by PeachPDF's CFF subsetter, not from
an upstream release, so the glyphs and subroutines it keeps are the ones that document used. It is bundled solely to make
the CFF subsetter tests deterministic across operating systems.
