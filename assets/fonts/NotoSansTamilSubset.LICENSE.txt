NotoSansTamilSubset.ttf is a subset (the consonants KA/TA/RA/SSA/HA - SSA and HA are Grantha-origin
letters Tamil borrows for Sanskrit loanwords, kept to exercise a real conjunct ligature - VIRAMA
(pulli), a pre-base vowel sign E, a post-base vowel sign AA, ANUSVARA, AAYTHAM, the independent
vowel A, common punctuation, digits, space, and the basic Latin alphabet) of the "Noto Sans Tamil"
font, Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/NotoSansTamil),
licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1). Instantiated to a single static
(Regular weight, normal width) instance from the upstream variable font before subsetting.

The font is bundled solely as a showcase/test asset so Tamil's Universal Shaping Engine (USE)
syllable reordering (issue #533, Phase 5c) - GSUB nukt/ccmp/locl/akhn/rphf/half/rkrf/cjct/abvs/
blws/pres/psts substitution and the resulting glyph reorder (pre-base matra movement) - renders
with real, readable Tamil glyphs and can be verified by rasterizing actual output, rather than
only unit-tested against synthetic byte-blob GSUB tables. Only the glyphs the showcase/tests
actually use are retained, with every GSUB/GPOS layout feature kept intact; the glyph outlines
themselves are unmodified.
