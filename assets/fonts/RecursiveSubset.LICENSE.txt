RecursiveSubset.ttf is a subset (basic Latin letters, digits, and common punctuation) of the
"Mono Casual Static" Regular instance of "Recursive", Copyright 2020 The Recursive Project
Authors (https://github.com/arrowtype/recursive), licensed under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1).

The font is bundled solely as a test/showcase asset to demonstrate and verify the CSS
`font-variant-alternates` property (specifically `styleset()`) and the `@font-feature-values`
at-rule against a real font with genuine numbered stylistic-set (`ssNN`) GSUB data - Recursive's
`ss01` (a -> a.simple, a single-story lowercase "a") and `ss02` (g -> g.simple, a single-story
lowercase "g") are real GSUB Single Substitution lookups, not a synthetic conformance font. The
subset was produced with fonttools, keeping every GSUB layout feature and glyph names unchanged.
See generate_recursive_subset.py.
