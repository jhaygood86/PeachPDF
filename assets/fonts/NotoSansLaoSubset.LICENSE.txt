NotoSansLaoSubset.ttf is a subset of the "Noto Sans Lao" font:

  Noto Sans Lao  Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors (https://github.com/notofonts/lao)

licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1). It was instantiated to a single static (Regular weight, normal width)
instance from the upstream variable font and cut down to the characters of the paragraph of the dictionary line breaking showcase and a
few punctuation marks, digits, space and the basic Latin alphabet, with every GSUB/GPOS layout feature kept intact; the glyph outlines
themselves are unmodified (generate_lao_subset.py, in this directory, makes it).

It is bundled solely as a showcase asset, so that the dictionary line breaking of Lao is shown with real, readable text. Nothing here
ships in the PeachPDF library or its NuGet packages.
