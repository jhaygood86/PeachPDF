HintingVariable.ttf, HintingVariableNoHvar.ttf, HintingVariableVertical.ttf, HintingVariableAvar2.ttf and the two golden files
HintingVariable.golden.json.gz and HintingVariableVariants.golden.json.gz are synthetic test data made by
generate_hinting_variable_fixtures.py in this directory: random polygons with random TrueType programs, random variation data
(tuples, control values, advances, metrics, axis maps) and variation tables made wrong on purpose. They contain no third-party
design and are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The golden files hold coordinates, advances and digests that FreeType computed from those fonts and from the variable fonts bundled
beside them (VariableTest.ttf and the others named in the script), and no font data. Each of those fonts keeps its own license notice
beside it.
