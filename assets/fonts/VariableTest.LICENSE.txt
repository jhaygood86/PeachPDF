VariableTest.ttf, VariableTestNoHvar.ttf and VariableTest.golden.json are synthetic test data made by
generate_variable_fixture.py in this directory: a handful of geometric glyphs on a weight and a width axis. They contain no
third-party design and are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
