NotoSansHebrewSubset.ttf is a subset (the Hebrew alphabet including final
forms, plus common punctuation, digits, and space) of the "Noto Sans Hebrew"
font, Copyright 2022 The Noto Project Authors, licensed under the SIL Open
Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL-1.1).

The font is bundled solely as a showcase/test asset so the bidirectional-text
(dir/bdo/bdi, direction/unicode-bidi, Unicode Bidi Algorithm) showcase and
tests render real Hebrew glyphs instead of .notdef boxes, without depending on
whatever fonts happen to be installed on the machine running them. Only the
glyphs the showcase HTML actually uses are retained; the outlines are
unmodified.
