VariableColorTest.ttf and VariableColorTest.golden.json are synthetic test data made by generate_variable_color_fixture.py in this
directory: a few geometric layer glyphs and colour glyphs (COLR version 1) with variable paints on a weight and a width axis. They contain no
third-party design and are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
