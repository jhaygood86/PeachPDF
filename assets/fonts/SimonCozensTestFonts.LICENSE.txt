SimonCozensCBDT.otf and SimonCozensCffAndSbix.otf are unmodified copies of CBDT.otf and CFF-and-SBIX.otf from
https://github.com/simoncozens/test-fonts ("Fonts for demonstrating and testing various parts of the OpenType
specification"), used only to test PeachPDF's CBDT/CBLC and sbix bitmap color-glyph rendering against real fonts.
They are not shipped in any PeachPDF package.
