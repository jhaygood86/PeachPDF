HintingGasp.ttf is a synthetic test font made by generate_hinting_gasp_fixtures.py in this directory: three rectangles with a
small TrueType program each, and a gasp table. It contains no third-party design and is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

HintingGasp.golden.json.gz is made by the same script: it holds the answers FreeType 2.14.3 gives about that font (and about variants of
it that differ only in their gasp table, which it holds as data), and coordinates it computed from the font's outlines. It contains no
other font.
